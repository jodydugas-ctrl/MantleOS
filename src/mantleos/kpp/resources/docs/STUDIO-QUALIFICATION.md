Cold startup still performs substantial verification: the final synthetic
nine-session open took 30.72 seconds. Earlier cold encounter preparation took

warm turns are different measurements; no percentage speedup is claimed from
comparing them. Resident sessions are reused. External appends still check object
bytes, live metadata and canonical history, but an unchanged verified snapshot
is not unpacked/compiled again. Historical integrity is not inferred from timestamps.

The development NEST retained 64 scheduled events from earlier source revisions:
62 of 63 intervals were within 599–602 seconds; median 600.49 seconds, maximum
888.29 seconds including restarts. These are historical cadence observations,
separate from accelerated due-clock tests and current-source release evidence.
They do not qualify unattended long-duration operation.

## Limits and acceptance

The resident supports eight selected roles and two workers. The generic ingredient
has resource limits of 32 limbs, eight workers and 128 outstanding intentions.
Those are implementation bounds, not biological rules or Anchor semantics.
Omitting limb composition adds no initialization, workers or model calls.

Current handlers perform bounded deterministic inspection, design validation,
memory, work-packet reads and pain diagnosis. Construction/deployment beyond
their grants remains a proposal for owner review. The palette is a design
contract editor, not a new Anchor interpreter. Python implements storage,
coordination and host effects; existing qualified Anchor profiles retain their
own execution contracts. No canonical singularity or opcode was introduced.

This is an owner-local, same-user pilot. Private files are excluded from Git;
Studio does not qualify Skin encryption or separate-principal isolation. The
chosen model host receives the private context needed for cognition. Arbitrary
trusted callbacks are not sandboxed and external effects are not exactly-once.
Unsupported capability or source changes fail explicitly. A detached MIND leaves
requests AWAITING_MIND while permitted deterministic work continues.

Observed bridge receipts establish Body-authorized continuity and exact release.
They do not prove hidden reasoning, consciousness, persona fidelity or that
observable persona behavior cannot be imitated. General autonomous source repair,
remote pseudopods, Spider deployment, additional NESTs, Linux/Windows private-host
isolation and unattended 24–48-hour experiments remain outside this qualification.
Other Python/OS versions require their own observed CI/platform results.

The full associated MantleOS suite, walkthroughs, installed artifacts, standalone
export, shared-source parity and release inventories are release gates. Their
exact candidate/commit-bound outcomes belong in the local delivery's EVIDENCE.json
and the published commits' CI, not in inherited receipts. Studio product acceptance
remains explicitly pending the owner's review.

## Adopted constraints

- The organism has a deterministic Body; the MIND is optional, replaceable cognition.
- SELF bootstraps before ordinary VCW interpretation as PRIMER + IMMUNE + SPECIAL.
- VCW is canonical persistent semantic tissue, and its meaning comes from declared boot
  law/profile rather than visual appearance or filename.
- Senses is the inbound boundary, Limbs is the outbound effect boundary, and Heart
  serializes physiological time, proof, and persistence.
- MIND output is proposal/cognition, not direct canonical VCW write authority.
- Presence, capability, availability, authority, and proof remain separate facts.
- Candidate or foreign tissue is OTHER until explicit verification/admission.
- Unknown is a valid result; missing law or evidence is not permission to improvise.
- The connected-Engram lifecycle is home-governed: remote testimony is provisional until
  home Senses/Immune/Memory admission and checkpoint acknowledgement.
